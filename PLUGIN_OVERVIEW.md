Manage the current BB browser tab from a Workbench button beside its address bar.

Grant or revoke site permissions, inspect native console errors, open the tab's DevTools, check graphics capabilities and prepare graphics profiles for the next desktop start. Native controls require the companion BB extension; an unpatched host retains read-only capability inspection.

Device grants are scoped to the tab and exact origin. MIDI grants are API-wide; choose individual audio/MIDI devices in the application. Changing permissions reloads the page.
